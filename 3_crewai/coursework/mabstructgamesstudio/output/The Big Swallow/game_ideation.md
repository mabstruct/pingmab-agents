# Game Concept Brief: The Big Swallow

## Game Title
**The Big Swallow**

---

## Short Idea Description
*The Big Swallow* is a fast-paced, physics-driven browser game where you play as a tiny, insatiable creature that grows by swallowing everything in its path — from crumbs and coins to cars, buildings, and eventually entire planets. But there's a twist: everything you swallow stays *inside* you, forming a chaotic living inventory that changes your abilities, weight, and behavior. Eat smart, digest strategically, and become big enough to swallow the game world itself.

---

## Core Idea

The game blends the addictive "eat-to-grow" loop of *Agar.io* and *Tasty Planet* with a unique **"living stomach" inventory system** inspired by roguelike deck-building. Unlike traditional grow-em-up games where swallowed objects simply add to a score, in *The Big Swallow* everything you consume becomes an active part of your character:

- Swallow a **magnet**? Nearby metal objects now drift toward your mouth.
- Swallow a **beehive**? Angry bees swarm around you, stunning smaller prey — but slowly sting your insides.
- Swallow a **balloon vendor**? You float higher and move faster, but become easier to knock around.

Players progress through escalating **scale tiers** (Sidewalk → Street → City → Continent → Planet → Cosmos), each unlocked by reaching a size threshold. Between tiers, players enter a short **"Digestion Phase"** — a strategic mini-round where they choose which stomach contents to *digest* (convert to permanent stat upgrades) and which to *keep* (retain as active abilities). This creates meaningful build decisions and endless replayability.

The tone is comedic, cartoonish, and absurd — think googly-eyed blob with a grin too big for its body, wobbling down a street while a fire truck siren wails from inside its belly.

---

## Key Features & Mechanics

### Core Mechanics
- **Swallow-to-Grow Loop** — Mouse/touch-controlled blob that can only swallow objects smaller than itself; size gates create natural progression and "forbidden fruit" tension (that bus you can't eat *yet*).
- **Living Stomach Inventory** — Every swallowed object appears visibly jiggling inside your translucent body and grants passive or active effects. Your stomach is your build.
- **Digestion Phase (Strategic Layer)** — Between scale tiers, choose to digest items for permanent upgrades (speed, jaw size, stomach capacity) or keep them for synergistic active powers. Deck-building meets snack-building.
- **Physics-Based Weight System** — What you eat affects how you move: heavy items slow you but make you immune to knockback; light items make you nimble but fragile. Balance your diet.
- **Indigestion & Hazards** — Some objects fight back from inside (angry dogs, dynamite, chili peppers) forcing you to **burp them out** as projectiles — turning hazards into weapons against rivals.

### Game Modes
- **Solo Campaign ("From Crumb to Cosmos")** — 6 escalating scale tiers with bosses (e.g., a rival swallower, a vacuum-cleaner mech, a hungry black hole finale).
- **Battle Royale Mode ("Swallow or Be Swallowed")** — 20-player .io-style arena where players can swallow each other; swallowed players briefly fight from *inside* the enemy's stomach for a chance to burst free.
- **Daily Buffet Challenge** — A daily seeded run with themed item pools (e.g., "Junk Food Friday") and global leaderboards.

### Engagement & Retention Features
- **Combo Chains** — Swallowing similar items in a row (all vehicles, all animals) builds a "Flavor Combo" multiplier for score and growth speed.
- **Belly Trophy Room** — A meta-collection of rare "Legendary Swallows" (the Eiffel Tower, a whale, the Moon) displayed in a persistent trophy gallery.
- **Cosmetic Progression** — Unlockable skins, mouth styles, burp sound packs, and stomach patterns — no pay-to-win, monetized via cosmetics and optional rewarded ads.
- **One-Click Shareable Moments** — Auto-generated GIF clips of your most absurd swallows ("You just swallowed a wedding. Share it?") for viral social spread.

### Technical & Accessibility
- **Instant Play** — No login required for first session; HTML5/WebGL, loads in under 5 seconds, runs on desktop and mobile browsers.
- **One-Input Control Scheme** — Fully playable with mouse or single touch; burp/dash mapped to click-hold for depth without complexity.
- **Session Design** — Solo runs of 8–12 minutes, battle royale matches of 5 minutes — perfect snackable sessions with a "one more run" hook.

## Why It Will Work
The eat-and-grow genre has proven mass appeal (*Agar.io*, *Hole.io*, *Katamari*), but no browser title has combined it with **build-crafting depth**. *The Big Swallow* gives casual players an instantly understandable fantasy — *eat everything* — while offering mid-core players roguelike strategy through the stomach-inventory system. The comedic tone and shareable absurd moments make it inherently viral, and the low-friction browser format ensures maximum reach.
